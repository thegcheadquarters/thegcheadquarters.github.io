What the role means

You hold leadership responsibilities without replacing the Owner, CEO, or Administrator, and you are recognized as an official GC leader.

Manager privileges

  1. Access to attendance records.
  2. Permission to send official announcements.
  3. Permission to organize GC events.
  4. Ability to recommend new members.
  5. Ability to report rule violations.
  6. Permission to lead meetings.
  7. Access to internal management discussions.
  8. Ability to submit improvement proposals.
  9. Permission to coordinate other staff.
  10. Recognition as an official GC leader.

Why the GC has a Manager

Leadership benefits
